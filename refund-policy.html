Refund Policy

Our 3-Day (72-hour) Money-Back Guarantee details, eligibility, and step-by-step refund process.

Last updated: October 10, 2026

3-Day Refund Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with your e-book, you may request a full refund within 3 days (72 hours) of your purchase date. Just email us. No complicated steps.

1. Who This Policy Covers

This Refund Policy applies to all digital products purchased from Smart Care E Books. Because our products are digital and delivered instantly, we cannot take them back once delivered. To keep things fair for you, we replace the right of return with a clear 3-day money-back window.

2. Refund Window

You can request a refund within 3 days (72 hours) from the date and time of your purchase, as shown on your payment confirmation or receipt.

Requests received after the 3-day window are not eligible for a refund, except where we are required by law to offer one, or where described in Section 5 below.

The refund is for the product price you paid. You do not need to give a detailed reason, but your feedback helps us improve.

3. How to Request a Refund

Send an email to support@smartcarevt.com within the 3-day window with the subject line "Refund Request" and include:

the full name and email address used for the purchase;

your order number or transaction ID;

the name of the e-book; and

an optional short reason for the request.

4. Review and Processing Time

Response: we will reply to your request within 2 business days.

Approval: eligible requests are approved and sent to our payment processor within 3 business days.

Money returned: refunds go back to the original payment method only. After we process the refund, it usually appears in 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank, card issuer, or payment provider.

Confirmation: you will receive an email when the refund has been issued.

When a refund is issued, your licence to the e-book ends and you must stop using it and delete all copies.

5. Special Situations We Will Always Help With

Even outside the usual conditions, please contact us within 3 days if:

Duplicate or accidental purchase: you were charged more than once for the same product.

Technical problem: you could not download, open, or read the file, and we were unable to fix the problem after reasonable attempts. We will first try to send you a working copy; if that does not solve it, we will refund you.

Wrong product: you received a file different from what you ordered.

Unauthorised charge: you did not make the purchase. Please also contact your bank.

6. Cases Where a Refund May Not Be Given

The request is made after the 3-day refund window has passed.

There is evidence that the e-book was shared, copied, resold, or distributed in breach of our Terms of Service.

The customer has a pattern of buying, receiving a refund, and keeping or reusing the content, or of repeated refund requests across multiple orders.

The refund request is a result of a change of mind about price after the window, or because of a lower price or promotion offered later.

Purchases made with a bundle or promotional discount are refunded only for the amount actually paid.

7. Fees and Taxes

Approved refunds cover the full amount you paid for the product. Where taxes were collected at checkout, they are returned together with the refund. Any currency conversion differences or bank fees applied by your own bank are outside our control and cannot be reimbursed by us.

8. Chargebacks

We ask that you contact us before opening a dispute with your bank or card issuer. A direct refund is faster and simpler than a chargeback. Where a chargeback is opened for an order that was delivered and is outside the refund window, we may submit evidence of delivery and access to the payment processor in response.

9. Your Legal Rights

This policy does not remove or limit any consumer rights you may have under the laws of your country that cannot be waived by agreement.

10. Contact for Refunds

Email: support@smartcarevt.com. Business hours: Monday to Saturday. Response time: within 2 business days.
