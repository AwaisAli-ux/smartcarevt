Privacy Policy

Our commitment to protecting your privacy, data security, and rights under applicable privacy laws.

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

Order information: your name, email address, billing country, and transaction details needed to complete and record your purchase.

Payment information: card and payment details are entered directly with our payment processors. We do not receive or store your full card number or security code.

Communication data: messages, emails, and support requests you send to us.

Technical data: IP address, device and browser type, and pages visited, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

To process payments and deliver your purchase;

To send order confirmations, download links, and receipts;

To provide customer support and handle refund requests;

To prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorised use of our products;

To improve our website, products, and services;

To send updates about new products only if you have agreed to receive them. You can unsubscribe at any time.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with:

payment processors, to take payment and process refunds;

email, hosting, and analytics providers who help us run the business and are required to protect your data;

authorities or legal advisers, when required by law or to protect our legal rights, including fraud and chargeback matters.

4. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to keep the site working, remember your choices, and understand how visitors use it. You can control cookies in your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect parts of the website.

5. Data Retention

We keep order and transaction records for as long as needed to provide the service, meet tax, accounting, and legal duties, and handle disputes. After that period, data is deleted or anonymised.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secure payment providers. No online system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, or to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email support@smartcarevt.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website and products are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International Users

We operate from Pakistan and serve customers in other countries, including the USA, Canada, and the UAE. By using our website you understand that your information may be processed in countries other than your own.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on our website with the updated date.

11. Contact

Smart Care E Books, Smart Care Tower, Blue Area, Islamabad, Pakistan. Email: support@smartcarevt.com.
